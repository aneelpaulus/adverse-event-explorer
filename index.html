Adverse Event Explorer

Compare the side effects people reported to the FDA for up to four drugs, or open one drug to see its trend over time.

Reports aren’t proof a drug caused anything. Widely used or publicized drugs collect more of them. Percentages are shares of each drug’s reports, not your chance of a side effect.

How it works

For each drug, the page counts openFDA reports that list it by ingredient, generic or product name, in any role (suspected cause or another drug the patient took). Side effects use the MedDRA terms reporters chose.
