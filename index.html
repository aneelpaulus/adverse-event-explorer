Adverse Event Explorer

Compare the side effects people have reported for different drugs, using the FDA Adverse Event Reporting System (FAERS) through the free openFDA service. Pick up to four drugs to see them side by side, or open one drug for its full profile and yearly report trend.

Read this first. These are voluntary reports of suspected side effects, not proof that a drug caused them. Drugs that are widely used, heavily publicized, or used in sicker patients collect more reports. Percentages show how often a side effect appears among one drug's reports, not how likely a patient is to experience it. For research and education only; not medical advice.

Terms that are probably not side effects

Reporters sometimes enter the disease being treated, its progression, or how the drug was used in the side effect field. These terms are set aside here instead of being counted as side effects. The rule is automatic, so check any that matter to you.

How it works. For each drug, the page asks openFDA for every report that lists the drug by active ingredient, generic name, or product name, in any role (the suspected cause or a drug the patient was also taking). It then counts reports per side effect and per year. Side effects use the MedDRA terms reporters chose.

Privacy. Your drug names are sent only to the public openFDA service. Nothing is stored. This tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FDA.
